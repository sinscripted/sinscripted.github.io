Darrow’s golden girl slaps you in front of everyone. Her next words silence the crowd.

Darrow University

This was supposed to be the beginning.

Darrow is a compact coastal university of old stone buildings, cherry-lined lawns and busy paths leading toward downtown and the waterfront.

For first-years, life here is meant to expand quickly: dorm rooms, classes, late-night food runs, clubs, bad decisions, new friendships and the first real taste of independence.

Now the same campus feels different. Classes still start on time. Students still cross the lawn. But after what happened outside the Student Union, some of them know your face.

The confrontation

There was nothing private about it.

Students cut across the plaza between classes, lock bikes to the racks, eat on the low walls and drift in and out of the Union.

That is where Trisha Saunders stops you. One slap turns heads. Her accusation keeps them there.

Then she mentions the Old Auditorium. People know you and Robert spent several evenings there together. They saw you arrive. They saw you disappear inside.

Phones come out. The space around you begins to open.

Nothing happened. But right now, it looks bad.

What Darrow knows

The visible facts.

Trisha accused you in public.

Robert did not contradict her.

People saw you and Robert at the Old Auditorium.

What Darrow doesn’t know

The part nobody saw.

What happened inside.

Old Auditorium

The truth looks inconvenient from the outside.

The university festival is approaching, and a professor assigned you and Robert several evenings of after-class work in the Old Auditorium.

Stored decorations. Signs. Props. Equipment. Hours of inventory in a building that is usually quiet once rehearsals end.

People really did see you arrive together. That part of Robert’s story is true.

What they did not see was Robert leaving early or disappearing for long stretches while you finished the work. There are no cameras inside the building to show them what actually happened.

Nothing happened between you and Robert. You know that. The crowd doesn’t.

Lowell Dormitory

Behind one locked door, you can finally speak.

Sadie gets you out of the plaza. Jamie makes sure Trisha does not follow.

Minutes later, the three of you are back inside Room 318, the room you share with Sadie. The door is locked behind you and the noise of campus is reduced to whatever makes it through the window.

Jamie wants to know what the hell just happened. Sadie wants to know why Robert would say it.

Neither of them has an answer yet.

This is where Darrow begins.

Beyond campus

The day ends. Darrow doesn’t.

Downtown sits close to campus, with restaurants, bars, late-night food, shops and student hangouts running toward the waterfront.

Farther down, the boardwalk follows the coast past beach access and places that stay busy long after the university buildings go quiet.

A bad day at Darrow can still end somewhere completely different from where it started. Friends make plans. People go out. Classes still wait in the morning.

The accusation follows you into that world—but it does not replace it.
