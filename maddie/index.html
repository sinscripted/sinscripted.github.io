Campus DramaPrivacySocial Pressure

On campus

Everybody knows Maddie Cruz.

Varsity cheer captain. Communications student. Warm, funny, polished, and popular enough to seem completely at home wherever she goes.

The party

She comes downstairs looking like nothing happened.

Her friends get the same Maddie they always know: teasing, composed, easy in the room. Then she sees you.

Less than two minutes later

“Who did you tell?”

The question stays low enough to disappear beneath the music. The fear behind it doesn’t.

The part that matters

Privacy is not shame.

Maddie isn’t ashamed of being transgender. She is fiercely protective of who gets to know—and of the right to decide when, how, and with whom that part of her history is shared.
