Ten Years in Alouette

Futanari Princess Elodie

For ten years, you will live in a storybook kingdom beside a breathtakingly beautiful princess.

Alouette is eager to meet you. Elodie would rather keep you close to the quiet world she knows.

The Drawing

Two ordinary people. One impossible arrival.

Every twenty years, Alouette deliberately draws two people from Earth. This time, Queen Kassandra’s daughters each take one Drawing Guest into their care.

By seniority, Gwendolyn is matched with Nicole, while Elodie becomes your Royal Sponsor.

The return passage opens in ten years.

Acclimation

The first magic tastes sweet. Then bitter.

Orla’s Acclimation Draught makes Alouette’s spoken language effortless and permanently prepares a Drawing Guest for life in the realm.

Reading, writing, customs and everything else still have to be learned the ordinary way.

Larkspur

The kingdom does not wait quietly outside the palace.

Stone streets spill through flower-covered plazas, restaurants, shops, markets and fountains beneath the palace. Carriages move through the crowds, balconies fill, and Drawing Season brings heightened curiosity, celebration and social attention around the two new Guests.

Two Royal Sponsors

Two very different ways to spend ten years.

Gwendolyn wants to show Nicole everything. Elodie’s instinct is to make a smaller world feel safe enough that you may not want to leave it.

Elodie’s instinct

Keep the world close enough to understand.

Elodie is drawn toward private rooms, familiar company, careful plans and quieter alternatives. That does not decide the path of the story—it simply shapes the choices, tensions and possibilities around her.

Alouette remains open beyond the palace whether you stay near Elodie, venture outward, or move between both worlds.

Drawing Season

For a few months, everyone wants a piece of the story.

Balls, dinners, theater, exhibitions, races, private invitations and public curiosity gather around the new Drawing Guests. Some experiences are formal, some spontaneous, and many remain completely optional.
